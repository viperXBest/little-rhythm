# Parent test, 3 Nov 2026

**Status: not yet run.** Results go below. Only first names or initials and with each
parent's OK; no emails, phone numbers, baby names or birth dates.

**Who:** 3–5 parents (pairs where possible) with a baby under a year, using the v1.1.0 APK
on their own Android phones, for an evening or longer.

## Consent note (read or send before installing)

> Little Rhythm is a hackathon project, not a released product. If you try it:
> - Your log and chats are stored on **demo-tier hosting** (Supabase, Frankfurt), with no
>   backups or uptime promise. When you're done, both parents delete their accounts in Settings; that removes
>   the family's data.
> - What you type or say in the chat goes to **Meta's Model API, hosted in the US**, to be
>   understood and answered. Voice clips are transcribed and not stored.
> - The app gives **no health advice**. For anything about your baby's health, call your
>   paediatrician, or the emergency number.
> - I'll quote what you tell me (first name or initials only) in the project's README,
>   only if you say yes.

## What to do (about 20 minutes, then normal use)

1. Install the APK, sign up, create the baby (a nickname is fine), and invite your
   partner with the code.
2. Log the next few feeds, sleeps and diapers: some by tap, some by voice or chat ("she
   drank 90 ml twenty minutes ago, she's asleep now").
3. Look at **Next up** after a few events.
4. Both parents answer the daily check-in.
5. Tell me (a message is enough):
   - What was useful? What was confusing or annoying?
   - Would you keep using it? Why or why not?
   - One sentence I may quote.
